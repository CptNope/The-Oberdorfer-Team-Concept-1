Buying, in order

Six steps, each with what you decide and what we do. Nothing happens out of order, and nothing happens without a note explaining it. The full buying guide

  1. 1

    Discover

    Which towns, what kind of house, what budget feels right.

    We: a first conversation, town notes, a lender introduction.
  2. 2

    Understand

    What the market is doing in the towns you chose.

    We: recent sales explained, a saved search tuned to you.
  3. 3

    Tour

    Seeing homes, and seeing past the staging.

    We: notes on each house, questions for the seller.
  4. 4

    Offer

    Price, terms and contingencies.

    We: comparable sales, options in writing, a recommendation.
  5. 5

    Close

    Inspection, appraisal, financing and paperwork.

    We: one timeline, every deadline, the attorney looped in.
  6. 6

    Move

    Keys, utilities and the first season.

    We: contractor names, tax dates, a check-in a year later.
