How a new agent gets a page

The team is built to grow without a rebuild.

  1. REWAP confirms the license.The agent joins under REWAP Brokerage LLC and the principal broker confirms the license number that appears on the page.
  2. One entry on the team roster.Name, role, license, towns, portrait and a short bio in their own words. The page, the card here and the links on their listings are generated from it.
  3. Listings match themselves.The IDX feed carries the MLS agent ID, so the agent's listings appear on their page and their name appears on each listing.
  4. Leads reach them directly.The agent is added as a user in Follow Up Boss. Their contact form and their listings' showing requests send person.assignedTo with their name, so the lead lands with them.
