The house

An 1838 side-gabled farmhouse: a center portico, two chimneys and a split-rail fence along the road.

Two rooms on either side of a center stair, as built, with a long kitchen added across the back in the 1950s.

The windows are later replacements; the floors and the front door are original. The barn foundation behind the house is all that remains of the outbuildings.
