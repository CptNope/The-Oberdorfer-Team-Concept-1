The house

Colonial Revival, 1932: a fanlight gable, a heated sunroom and the original built-ins in the dining room.

A symmetrical front, black shutters and an enclosed porch that the current owners use as a mudroom in winter and a reading room the rest of the year.

The dining room keeps its corner cupboards. The sunroom on the south side was added in the 1960s and insulated in 2021.
