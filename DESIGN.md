# Design System: The Oberdorfer Team

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quarterly on New England Stock"**

The Oberdorfer Team is presented the way an independent architecture quarterly presents a building: colored paper stocks own whole spreads, a serif carries the reading and the headlines, a grotesk on its width axis carries every label and number, and photographs are cropped like measured drawings and captioned like plates. Color is material, not decoration. Shutter green is the cover and every chapter opener; cool plaster is every reading page; oak is a second stock reserved for selling, photography and affiliation; brick is a spot ink printed sparingly on top.

Density is editorial, not portal-like. A dense passage (a fact table, a list of rows) is followed by a quiet one (a full-bleed photograph, a single statement, white space). Structure comes from a 12-column grid, square corners and hairline rules, never from shadows, cards or rounded containers. A sticky running folio tracks where the reader is, like the running head of a printed issue.

The system rejects the brokerage default (aerial hero, search bar, listing grid), cream-and-serif softness, roof/key/pin iconography, and canned REALTOR template patterns. Every photograph is a labeled, credited sample until the team supplies real work.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Full-bleed color stocks (green, plaster, oak) instead of floating cards
- Inria Serif for display and reading; Archivo on its width axis (82 / 100 / 116) for labels, data and prices
- Square corners everywhere; 1px hairlines separate, a 2px ink rule starts a list or table
- Brick as spot ink: one primary action per view, figure and chapter numbers on plaster
- A real paper grain blended into every stock
- Fig.-numbered captions, folio line, chapter numerals: the vocabulary of a printed issue
- Motion is a page turn: a masked wipe, a settle, then stillness

## Colors

A palette of New England building materials, used as paper stocks and inks: three stocks own surfaces, one spot ink marks action, and a small set of inks is tuned per stock.

### Primary
- **Shutter Green** (stock-green): The signature stock. Cover, every green chapter opener, colophon/footer, the contents-spread corner tab and stamp, green panels. Roughly 40% of surface on a typical page.
- **Clay** (on-green-accent): Brick's tint, the accent ink that replaces brick on green: chapter numerals, contents-index numerals on the cover, text links and focus rings on green.
- **Lichen** (on-green-muted): Secondary ink on green: captions, colophon headings, the brokerage line on green.
- **Green Plaster Ink** (on-green-body): Body copy in the footer/colophon and footer legal on green.

### Secondary
- **Brick** (spot-brick): Spot ink on plaster. The one primary action per view, figure/step/chapter numerals on plaster, the folio's chapter number, error state, focus ring, selection highlight. Stays near 3% of surface.
- **Pressed Brick** (brick-pressed): Hover/pressed state of the brick button.
- **Ink on Brick** (ink-on-brick): A warmer, lighter plaster used for text on brick, for crisper contrast than plaster alone.

### Tertiary
- **Oak** (stock-oak): The second stock. Affiliation and photography chapter openers, the pasted card on full-photo openers, the home-valuation panel. Roughly 8% of surface.
- **Deep Ink** (ink-deep): The only text ink on oak; also the phone-mock bezel and code/payload panels.
- **Oak Shadow** (on-oak-muted): Captions on oak.
- **Oak Field** (field-on-oak) and **Oak Field Edge** (field-border-on-oak): Input fill and border inside the oak valuation panel.

### Neutral
- **Plaster** (stock-plaster): Every reading page and the folio bar. A cool off-white, never cream.
- **Paper** (paper): Inset panels on plaster: input fills, specimen faces, lockup stages, the showing-request panel, "after" rewrites.
- **Recessed Plaster** (plaster-recessed): Disabled buttons, "before" rewrites, word chips, schematic blocks.
- **Slate** (ink): Body ink on plaster, outlined buttons, the 2px rule that opens lists and tables, map price markers, toasts.
- **Stone** (ink-muted): Secondary text on plaster: notes, captions, meta labels, table headers, attribution lines.
- **Hairline** (rule) and **Hairline on Green** (rule-on-green): 1px separators on plaster and on green.
- **Strong Rule** (rule-strong): Fact-list dividers, chip outlines, field borders, the bottom-sheet grab handle. Decorative grade only (see Do's and Don'ts).

### Named Rules
**The Stock Rule.** Color fills whole bands edge to edge. Green, oak and plaster are surfaces a region is printed on, never a rounded card floating on a page.

**The Spot Ink Rule.** Brick is printed on plaster or paper only, at about 3% of a page: one brick button per view, numerals, error marks. On green, brick becomes clay. On oak, nothing is brick except the valuation panel's submit button.

**The Tested Pairs Rule.** Text uses only the pairs in the table above. A pair that is not listed is not allowed, however good it looks.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inria Serif (with Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Georgia)
**Body Font:** Inria Serif (same family; Regular for reading, Bold for headlines, Light only for chapter numerals, Italic for a property's character line)
**Label/Data Font:** Archivo, variable width axis 62–125 (with Helvetica Neue, Segoe UI, Arial)

**Character:** A sharp, slightly wedged contemporary serif does the talking; a 19th-century-wood-type grotesk narrows to label captions and widens to set prices like a ledger. One serif, one grotesk, and the width axis does the work a third family would otherwise do.

- **Statement** (Regular 400, clamp 30–58px, line 1.08, max 22ch): Pull statements; emphasis in brick italic.

- **Address** (Bold 700, clamp 26–40px featured; 22px in compact cards; 20px in result rows): Property addresses.

- **Data** (Archivo, width 100, 15px, tabular): Beds/baths/area/lot lines, inputs (16px), table cells.

### Named Rules
**The Width Axis Rule.** Archivo's width encodes role: 82 for labels, captions, meta and the brokerage line; 88–92 for controls, notes and sans headings; 100 for data and inputs; 108–116 for fact figures and prices. Never introduce a third family.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Prices, square footage, dates, counts and every numeral column use Archivo with tabular figures, so lists of homes align like a ledger.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Capitals belong to the wordmark and short meta labels. Headlines, buttons and navigation stay in sentence case.

### WordPress mapping
`fontFamilies`: `inria-serif` (300/400/700 + italics) and `archivo` (variable, `wdth` 62–125, `wght` 100–900), self-hosted in the theme. `fontSizes` slugs: `display`, `headline-1`, `headline-2`, `statement`, `title`, `lead`, `body`, `note` (14px), `meta` (13px), `price`, using the clamp values above as fluid sizes. Width roles ship as block-style variations (`is-style-label`, `is-style-price`, `is-style-data`) setting `font-stretch`.

## Layout

- **Grid:** 12 columns, `minmax(0, 1fr)`, column gap `gap` (16–32px). Side margins `gutter` (16–90px, about 3.56% of viewport, matching the cover's inset). 16px is the phone floor.
- **Reading page:** each sub-section is a 4 + 8 split: title (and note) in columns 1–4, body in 5–12; `sub-wide` spans all 12 for galleries, cards, schematics. Sub-sections are separated by a hairline and `section-block` (36–72px) padding; pages open with `page-block` (56–112px).
- **Asymmetry:** prefer 7 + 5 and 8 + 4 splits (featured property 8 + 4, property detail 8 + 4 with a sticky side panel, motion and affiliation 7 + 5, contents spread 1 + 1). Centered layouts are reserved for forms and the colophon.
- **Chapter openers:** full-bleed stock bands with `opener-block` (56–132px) padding. Split: text in columns 1–6, photograph in 8–12 bleeding off the right edge (4:5, max 78vh). Band: numeral in 1–5, title and deck in 6–12, then a 32:9 panoramic strip bleeding both edges (16:9 under 900px). Full: a 56vw photograph (420–980px) with an oak card pasted over its lower-left edge (max 720px / 62vw, pulled up 120–260px).
- **Running folio:** sticky 44px plaster bar with a hairline below, carrying the publication name left and the current chapter number and title right. Anchor targets get a 56px scroll margin.
- **Rhythm:** dense passages alternate with quiet ones: a full photograph, a single statement, white space.
- **Breakpoints:** 1100px (cover switches from measured frame to stacked issue cover; journey drops to 3 columns), 900px (all splits stack to one column; cards to 2), 640px (rows, forms and pairs stack), 560px (cards to 1; masthead stacks; contents list to one column), 520px (stock swatches to 1).
- **Phone:** photography first, sticky actions at the bottom (two 44px targets, clear of the home indicator), filters in a bottom sheet over a 45% ink scrim, map and list swapped by one segmented toggle.

### Named Rules
**The Folio Rule.** Every long page carries a running folio that names where the reader is (chapter, town or listing). It is a navigation aid, not a second header: one line, 13px, no logo.

**The One Strong Move Rule.** Each screen has one dominant move: a stock band, a photograph or a statement. Never two competing heroes.

### WordPress mapping
`layout.contentSize` ≈ 64ch reading column, `wideSize` = full grid width inside `gutter`. `spacingSizes` slugs: `gutter`, `gap`, `page-block`, `section-block`, `opener-block`, `panel`. Openers ship as block patterns (`opener-split`, `opener-band`, `opener-full`).

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. There are no structural shadows anywhere: depth comes from changing stock (plaster to green to oak), from inset paper panels on plaster, and from a real paper grain. The grain is an 800px photographic paper texture tiled at 400px and blended `soft-light`: full strength (`grain.jpg`) on plaster, half strength (`grain-soft.jpg`) on green and oak stocks, the colophon, green/oak panels and the contents-spread tab, stamp and caption bar, so the dark stocks read at printed-board amplitude. Translucent ink scrims are allowed only where text or a control must sit on a photograph (the save toggle at 62% deep ink, the phone sheet scrim at 45%).

### Named Rules
**The Printed Stock Rule.** Surfaces are paper, not glass. No drop shadows, no blur, no gradients; a region gets depth by being printed on a different stock.

**The Grain Strength Rule.** Grain is always present on stocks, full on plaster and half on green and oak. It never sits on photographs or inside inputs.

## Shapes

Square corners everywhere (0px): buttons, inputs, panels, tags, chips, photographs, map markers. Lines do the structural work: 1px hairlines (`rule`) separate rows, sub-sections and folio; a 2px slate rule opens a list, journey, table, pillar or Field Notes card; a 3px green top rule marks a confirmation or an approved rewrite; a 3px inset brick bar marks a field in error. The only curves are illustrative: the phone-mock bezel (38px), its grab handle and range thumbs, and map roads.

### Named Rules
**The Square Corner Rule.** Nothing in the interface is rounded. A rounded corner is a sign the component came from somewhere else.

**The Rules Not Boxes Rule.** A list, table or set begins with a 2px slate rule and divides with 1px hairlines. Rules replace boxes.

## Components

### Wordmark and affiliation lockups
Lettered like an architect's title block: "The Oberdorfer Team" in Inria Serif Regular, uppercase, tracked 0.38em (0.32em / 0.26em on the smallest stacked covers), in shutter green on plaster or plaster on green. No symbol.
- **Primary lockup:** wordmark over "at REWAP Brokerage LLC" in Archivo width 82, sentence case, +0.01em, 60% of the wordmark size, never below 12px on screen or 7pt in print, never letterspaced to match.
- **Inline lockup:** wordmark and brokerage line on one baseline (gap 0.9em, brokerage at 62%), for navigation; on phones the brokerage line moves under the name.
- **Stacked lockup:** used in the colophon/footer, brokerage line in lichen on green.
- **Compact form:** "OBERDORFER" alone, same tracking, for tight headers and social bios. Minimum full wordmark 130px wide / 1.3in.
- **Square avatar:** a single serif "O" on shutter green, only for favicons and social avatars.
- **Clear space:** one cap height on every side.

### Running folio

### Toast

### Motion
- **Tokens:** `ease-settle` cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1) for everything; `dur-quick` 160ms (hover, press, save); `dur-base` 320ms (folio swap, sheets, menus, toast); `dur-reveal` 900ms (opening photographs).
- **Masked reveal:** an opener photograph wipes in from the left (clip-path inset 0 100% 0 0 → 0) over 900ms while the image settles from 105% to 100% over 1400ms. Content is visible at rest; reveals arm only after the first scroll and only for figures more than 1.05 viewport heights below, firing once at 18% visibility. The page is complete without it.
- **Hover:** underlines draw, buttons darken. Images never zoom on hover.
- **Reduced motion:** every reveal, slide and swap becomes an instant change; smooth scrolling is turned off.

### Named Rules
**The Affiliation Rule.** The Oberdorfer Team is the consumer brand; REWAP Brokerage LLC is the brokerage of record. The brokerage appears typographically only, in the lockup's Archivo line, in the header of every page, every agent bio, every listing attribution ("Listed by The Oberdorfer Team at REWAP Brokerage LLC · MLS PIN #" for own listings; "Listing courtesy of [Listing Brokerage]" for IDX), under every contact form, and in the footer with Equal Housing Opportunity. It is never larger than the team name, never letterspaced to match it, and the team is never presented as the brokerage.

**The Labeled Sample Rule.** Until real material exists, every photograph carries a credited "Sample photograph" caption and every listing, article or slot carries a visible sample tag. Nothing is presented as real that is not.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** print regions on stock: shutter green for covers, openers and footers; plaster for reading; oak for selling, photography and affiliation.
- **Do** keep brick to one primary button per view plus numerals, on plaster or paper only; switch to clay on green.
- **Do** use only the tested contrast pairs; body text never drops below 17px.
- **Do** set every price and numeral in Archivo with tabular figures, prices at width 116.
- **Do** caption every photograph with a figure number, what it shows, where, and who made it.
- **Do** keep a visible focus ring on every control: 2px brick at 3px offset on plaster, clay on every green surface (cover, panels, colophon), deep ink on oak.
- **Do** keep touch targets at least 44 × 44px and controls 48px tall.
- **Do** show the team-and-brokerage lockup in every header and the affiliation statement in every footer.
- **Do** give every map a list equivalent and a text address.
- **Do** write alt text that describes the house or place; hide decorative numerals and textures from screen readers.

### Don't:
- **Don't** round corners, add drop shadows, or float a colored card on a page.
- **Don't** set text in plaster on oak (2.42:1), brick on oak (2.32:1) or brick on green (1.79:1).
- **Don't** use rule-strong (#8D938F, 2.77:1 on paper) as the only boundary of an interactive control; a field or chip outline must reach 3:1 against its surroundings.
- **Don't** place text directly on a photograph without a stock panel or a measured scrim.
- **Don't** add roof, key, pin or house symbols, generic monograms, red barns, colonial clip art or faux handwritten type.
- **Don't** use Inter, Poppins or Montserrat, or a third type family.
- **Don't** set headlines, buttons or navigation in capitals; capitals are for the wordmark and short meta labels.
- **Don't** zoom images on hover, bounce, or float anything; motion is a wipe, a settle, then stillness.
- **Don't** make the brokerage line larger than the team name, letterspace it to match, or present the team as the brokerage.
- **Don't** present a sample photograph, listing, statistic or testimonial as real.
- **Don't** describe who should live somewhere; describe homes and places.
