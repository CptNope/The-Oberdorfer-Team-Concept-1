One platform, two brands

From chapter 12. Every form on the website shows the Follow Up Boss event it would send, and sends nothing.

The team site and a future REWAP site share data without duplicating it. A vendor-neutral IDX adapter turns the MLS PIN feed into one property record per listing. Every lead reaches Follow Up Boss as an event carrying the person, the house or search, the page and the campaign it came from, with the brokerage attached.

Ground rules

Team, then brokerage
The Oberdorfer Team is the consumer brand; REWAP Brokerage LLC is the brokerage of record. Both appear on every page, listing and form, and the brokerage line is never larger than the team name.
Nothing invented
No testimonials, sales figures, awards, license numbers or response times. Unknowns are bracketed placeholders, like [license #].
Samples are labeled
Every listing, price and story is a sample; every photograph is a credited Unsplash sample. Listing interiors are not of the listed house, and the gallery says so.
Fair housing
We describe homes and places, never who should live in them. Equal Housing Opportunity in every footer.
Access
WCAG 2.1 AA: tested color pairs only, visible focus, 44px targets, labeled fields, reduced motion respected.

Before launch

What the team and REWAP supply.

People
Portraits, bios, Kait's role and license details, direct phone numbers and a reply time the team can keep.
Brokerage
REWAP's address and license block, required disclosures, and the principal broker's sign-off on footer language.
Follow Up Boss
Register the site as a system, create the API key, name the source, set the lead flow and action plans, and create the Brokerage custom field. The checklist
Data
The IDX vendor, the MLS PIN disclaimer text, and the monthly market figures for the town pages.
Photography
A real shoot to replace every sample. The shot list is in chapter 07 of the brand book.
Service commitments
The copy carries the brand book's proposed commitments: answering their own phones, plans in writing, a weekly written update. The team confirms each one, or it comes out.
Writing
Town pages for the launch towns, written and fact-checked by the team; the first month of Field Notes.
