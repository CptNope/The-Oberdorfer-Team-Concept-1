# Surface: Brand book (brand-book.html; was index.html until the concept overview took the root)

Scope: the first deliverable — a digital brand book for The Oberdorfer Team, presented to Brandon and Kait Oberdorfer at an in-person meeting. Visitor mode: Persuade (pitch-led), with full tokens and rules underneath so it also works as the build reference for the WordPress theme.

Audience and job: the team leads decide whether this is the brand they want; later, Jeremy and developers implement from it.
Action: approve the direction; the in-page action is "Open the book" into the contents spread, then chapter-by-chapter reading.
Proof/content: brand strategy, voice, identity, type, color, photography, layout, UI patterns (incl. property cards, community, buying/selling), motion, accessibility, brokerage affiliation. No real photos, testimonials or stats exist: all photography is verified Unsplash stock, captioned as sample; no invented claims.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md brand commitments; REWAP appears typographically only; WCAG 2.1 AA.

Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/comp-a.png (opener). Second screen follows .impeccable/mocks/comp-b.png (contents spread). User chose "Combine A + B".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The brand book opens as an independent architecture quarterly printed on New England material stock: shutter-green cover, plaster pages, brick spot ink. It refuses the brokerage default (aerial hero, search bar, listing grid) and refuses cream-and-serif softness by letting colored stock own whole spreads.

OWN-WORLD: Full palette as paper stocks. Shutter green #23382F owns the cover and every chapter opener; cool plaster #E7E5DF pages; slate ink #2A3136; brick #A3452E spot ink for numerals, figure numbers and the one primary action; oak #B98D5A as a second stock for the selling and affiliation openers. Inria Serif for display and reading; Archivo on its width axis (condensed for labels and captions, wide tabular for prices and data). Square corners, hairline rules, Fig.-numbered captions, folio lines.

STORY: Brandon and Kait see their team as a publication-worthy local brand, understand who it serves and how it speaks, see the team-to-brokerage relationship designed rather than bolted on, and approve the system.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plaster masthead: wordmark left in wide tracked serif caps over "at REWAP Brokerage LLC"; issue line right. A shutter-green cover panel at ~93% width: left, "Home is personal." in huge serif over two lines, standfirst, brick "Open the book" button (primary action); right, a tall cropped clapboard photograph (37% width) with Fig. 01 caption; bottom hairline and a one-row contents index 01–12.

FORM: The Quarterly (architecture quarterly), first on the ordered grounded list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK; the roll had assigned The House Record, user chose the pick), seed key 459aece9. Signature interaction: a running folio that tracks the current chapter number and title as the reader moves through stock-colored chapter openers, each opening photograph revealed by a clean masked wipe.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

- Cover photograph: verified Unsplash stock per the user's "Verified stock, labeled" answer; best available match (clapboard, slate, black shutters), mild warm white balance.
