At the door

The sign-in sends a Visited Open House event to Follow Up Boss, which starts the team's open-house follow-up plan.

Whoever is hosting will hand you the Massachusetts relationship disclosure, the state form that explains who represents whom, and ask whether you're already working with an agent. If you are, we'll talk with them, not around them.

Signing in is optional. If you do, you'll get our written notes on the house: what we saw in the basement and the attic, what's been updated and when, and what similar houses nearby sold for.
