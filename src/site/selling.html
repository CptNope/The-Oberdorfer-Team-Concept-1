What Massachusetts asks of sellers

A plain summary. Your attorney confirms what applies to your house.

Smoke and CO certificate
Before the sale closes, the local fire department inspects the smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and issues a certificate. We schedule it early; some towns book out.
Title 5 inspection
If the house is on a septic system, it needs a Title 5 inspection, generally within the two years before the sale. A failed system changes the sale, so we find out first.
Lead paint notification
For houses built before 1978, buyers receive the state's lead paint notification and the federal disclosure, and you share what you know.
Your attorney
Sellers in Massachusetts usually hire their own attorney to review the Purchase and Sale agreement and prepare the deed. We'll introduce you to a few.
